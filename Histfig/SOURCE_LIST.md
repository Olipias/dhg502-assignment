# Source List — Lin Huiyin Corpus (Assignment 2, Part 1)

**Figure:** 林徽因 Lin Huiyin (1904–1955)
**Prepared:** 10 November 2026
**Total size:** 1,189,674 characters (1,171,569 characters excluding whitespace; 1,017,510 Chinese characters), 11 UTF-8 `.txt` files.

The corpus contains (a) Lin Huiyin's own literary and architectural writings, (b) letters written by her, (c) a biography and a family-letter collection that describe her, and (d) architectural writings by her husband and collaborator Liang Sicheng that belong to the same scholarly project. Every file is a digitised published text, not a text generated for this assignment.

---

## 1. Files

### 01. `01_lin_huiyin_wenji_235k.txt`
- **Title:** 《林徽因经典全集》 (Lin Huiyin: Classic Collected Works)
- **Author:** 林徽因 Lin Huiyin
- **Contents:** poems, essays, short stories, the play 《梅真同他们》, selected letters, and architectural articles (《平郊建筑杂录》, 《晋汾古建筑预查纪略》, 《清式营造则例》绪论, 《我们的首都》, etc.).
- **Print edition:** a widely circulated popular compilation; no single authoritative print edition can be identified from the file itself. Treated as a **core collection**, not as a scholarly critical edition.
- **Digital source:** `https://github.com/hankinghu/literature-books/blob/master/林徽因经典全集.txt` (file originally distributed by 小说TXT天堂 / xiaoshuotxt.com).
- **Access date:** 10 November 2026.
- **Size:** 222,762 characters.
- **Reliability notes:** cross-checked on sampled works against files 05, 08, 09 and 10. Website watermarks, chapter-numbering artefacts, and editorial notes in 【】 were removed. A short biography appended by the e-book compiler is retained at the end as an editorial appendix; it is **not** Lin Huiyin's own writing.

## 2. Why these texts?

- **Her own voice:** files 01, 05, 08, 09, 10 and 11 contain Lin Huiyin's poems, essays, fiction, drama, letters and architectural writings. This is the part of the corpus that the RAG system should use when a question asks what Lin Huiyin thought, wrote or designed.
- **Contemporary and biographical context:** file 02 is a narrative biography; file 06 contains Liang Qichao's family letters, which mention Lin Huiyin directly and document the families' expectations around her marriage and study abroad.
- **The architectural partnership:** files 03, 04 and 07 are by Liang Sicheng, her husband and closest collaborator. They are necessary for questions about the buildings, surveys and conservation work in which Lin Huiyin participated, and they supply the technical vocabulary that her own architectural essays assume.

The corpus deliberately includes some overlap between editions. Overlap is useful for the in-class RAG exercise: if two editions disagree, the group can compare them against the print source and judge which answer is reliable. It also means that the 1-million-character target is met without padding with unrelated material.

## 3. Verification performed

1. **Edition comparison.** Sampled passages were compared across independent editions:
   - 《山西通信》 and 《窗子以外》 from file 01 vs. the Fanqie edition: character-level similarity ≈ 0.98–0.99 after removing punctuation differences.
   - 《那一晚》 and 《笑》 from file 01 vs. file 05 / the Fanqie edition: similarity ≈ 0.97–0.99.
   - Letters to 沈从文 and 胡适 in file 08 vs. the corresponding sections of file 01: the same text, with minor punctuation variants.
2. **Structural checks.** Chapter titles and order were checked against the tables of contents of the digital editions. Locked or missing chapters are listed above, not silently omitted.
3. **Watermark and artefact removal.** Website URLs, advertisements and embedded watermarks were removed from files 01, 02 and 03. Editorial notes in 【】 were removed only from file 01; footnote markers in file 05 are part of the printed text and were retained.
4. **Font de-obfuscation.** The 番茄小说 texts (files 04, 05, 06) use a subset font that remaps Chinese characters to private-use code points. The mapping was recovered by matching each glyph against Source Han Sans SC; the decoded text was then sampled against independent editions. Residual `□` characters in the inscriptions in files 01, 02, 03 and 04 are **original lacunae in the stone inscriptions**, not decoding failures, except where noted.
5. **Removed material.** Three kinds of material were deliberately excluded:
   - truncated previews from 番茄小说 that were shorter than the real chapter (a 作品集 file was removed for this reason);
   - one mislabeled chapter in the 七猫 书信集 whose content is by Wang Zengqi, not Lin Huiyin;
   - website navigation and part-heading pages that contained no article text.

## 4. Copyright and use

Lin Huiyin died in 1955. Under Chinese copyright law her own works are in the public domain (life + 50 years). Liang Sicheng died in 1972; his works are also in the public domain in China. The modern editorial matter, translations, prefaces and the biography by Lin Shan may still be protected; they are included here for non-commercial coursework and text-mining/retrieval experiments, with the source and edition recorded above. The files should not be redistributed outside the course.

---

## 5. Known limitations

- The corpus is **not** a critical edition. File 01 is a popular compilation, and files 04, 05 and 06 are platform editions with some chapters locked.
- File 02 is a literary biography with dramatised passages; it should not be used as the sole authority for a factual claim.
- Files 04, 05 and 06 are **partial**: the missing chapters are identified in the entries above.
- There is overlap between files 01, 05, 10 and 11. This is intentional for cross-checking, but it means the corpus contains duplicate passages. For retrieval experiments, deduplicate by paragraph if a cleaner index is needed.
- The text is simplified Chinese, UTF-8, with punctuation normalised to current usage in some editions. Variant characters (e.g. 徽音/徽因) are retained as printed.
