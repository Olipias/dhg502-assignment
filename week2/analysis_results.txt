0. Method
------------------------------------------------

POLITY) and a speech flag, then put in one of four buckets:
  君臣同现   a RULER word and a MINISTER word occur together
  君臣言论   direct speech by a ruler or minister, no co-occurrence
  政治·其他  a POLITY word, or merely a ruler/minister mention
  其他       none of the above
Political = the first three buckets.

Caveats
------------------------------------------------
* Classical Chinese has no word spacing; bigrams are an approximation
  of words, and overlapping names (子胥 in 伍子胥) inflate raw counts.
* Keyword tagging can miss irony or count a word in a non-political
  sense (e.g. 师 in 农师, 使 'send' vs 'envoy'). Numbers are a proxy,
  good for a rough share, not for a sentence-by-sentence judgement.
* The text is a simplified-character edition with a few OCR gaps.
